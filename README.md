<h1>UK Energy Expenditure Analysis</h1>

<h2>Description</h2>
Completed a simulated data analysis project for Electricité Verte, a French renewable energy supplier exploring expansion into the UK market. Analysed two years of customer energy expenditure data (2020-2021) across 100 households to provide insights to support business planning.

<h2>Skills demonstrated</h2>

- <b>Data Cleaning</b> 
- <b>Data Integration</b>
- <b>Data Validation</b>
- <b>Data reporting</b>
- <b>Business Insights</b>
- <b>Excel formulas</b>

<h2>Project</h2>

- <b>Combined two datasets using XLOOKUP to match customer information with energy readings. </b>
- <b>Cleaned and validated data, correcting negative values and addressing 281 missing gas readings.</b>
- <b>Used Excel formulas, sorting and filtering to calculate annual expenditure, averages and yearly changes.</b>
- <b>Identified a 5.99% increase in gas expenditure and a 3.75% increase in electricity expenditure between 2020 and 2021.</b>
